# Dataset `final_seed_2025`

The ledger of the application and of the demonstrations: 25,000 invoices from 1,250 vendors over the financial year 2025-26, the fourteen anomaly classes (141 invoices each) and five hard-negative types (60 each) of the benchmark, about 40% of the eligible anomalous invoices carrying one to three further violations (compound frauds, so that flagged invoices score on more than the 70 or 50 of a single finding), four pairs of shell vendors and six approver rings. `labels.csv` has a column `secondary_class_ids` for the classes added to a compound fraud; `manifest.json` lists the rings.

Generated by `python -m auditiq_bench --only final_seed_2025` (generator 1.0, seed 2025, policy v2.5). The same command gives byte-identical files; `manifest.json` holds their SHA-256 hashes. Everything is synthetic: no vendor, person or invoice is real.

## Files

| File | Rows | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `vendor_master.csv` | 1,250 | one row per vendor: name, GSTIN, category, blacklist flag, registration status |
| `purchase_orders.csv` | 24,624 | what was ordered: amount, quantity, unit price, description |
| `goods_receipts.csv` | 24,577 | what was received against a PO: quantities and the receiver's remarks |
| `invoices.csv` | 25,000 | what the vendor billed, with its PO and GRN references |
| `approval_logs.csv` | 28,290 | who forwarded or approved each invoice, with role and time |
| `labels.csv` | 25,000 | ground truth of every invoice; never read by the pipeline |
| `eval_subset.csv` | 3,657 | the invoices the LLM configurations audit, with weights |
| `manifest.json` | | seed, versions, counts, file hashes |

The five record tables hold 103,741 records. Their columns are described in `docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`; they load into the database with `python -m auditiq setup --dataset <folder>`.
Invoices are dated 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31.

## Planted collusion

The rings below were planted; the detector never reads them. An approver ring: the approver passes the listed invoices of its vendors above the limit of the role (Rule 5.1.1), among ordinary invoices of the same vendors. ## Evaluation subset

| Stratum | Invoices | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| duplicate_original | 282 | 1.000 |
| hard_negative | 300 | 1.000 |
| anomalous | 2,275 | 1.000 |
| ordinary | 800 | 27.679 |

The weights sum to 25,000, the number of invoices. The controls and the classical detectors are evaluated on the whole ledger; the LLM configurations audit this subset, with the whole ledger as the vendor history, and their rates are computed with the weights.
