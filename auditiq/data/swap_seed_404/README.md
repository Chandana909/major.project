# Dataset `swap_seed_404`

The policy-swap ledger. 150 boundary invoices violate one rule under policy v2.5 and none under v2.6, which differs in the amount tolerance, the daily invoice count and the Manager approval limit; the other 4,850 invoices are clean under both. It tests that a change of policy changes the outcome with no change to the pipeline.

Generated by `python -m auditiq_bench --only swap_seed_404` (generator 1.0, seed 404, policy v2.5). The same command gives byte-identical files; `manifest.json` holds their SHA-256 hashes. Everything is synthetic: no vendor, person or invoice is real.

## Files

| File | Rows | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `vendor_master.csv` | 250 | one row per vendor: name, GSTIN, category, blacklist flag, registration status |
| `purchase_orders.csv` | 5,000 | what was ordered: amount, quantity, unit price, description |
| `goods_receipts.csv` | 5,000 | what was received against a PO: quantities and the receiver's remarks |
| `invoices.csv` | 5,000 | what the vendor billed, with its PO and GRN references |
| `approval_logs.csv` | 5,654 | who forwarded or approved each invoice, with role and time |
| `labels.csv` | 5,000 | ground truth of every invoice; never read by the pipeline |
| `eval_subset.csv` | 300 | the invoices the LLM configurations audit, with weights |
| `manifest.json` | | seed, versions, counts, file hashes |

The five record tables hold 20,904 records. Their columns are described in `docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`; they load into the database with `python -m auditiq setup --dataset <folder>`.
Invoices are dated 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31.

## Ground truth

150 of the 5,000 invoices are anomalous (3.0%), 0 are hard negatives and 4,850 are ordinary clean invoices.

| Class | Anomaly | Rule(s) | Decided by | Severity | Invoices |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Invoice amount differs from PO amount beyond the tolerance | 2.1.1 | a control | high | 50 |
| R6 | Same-day invoice splitting above the daily count | 4.1.1 | a control | high | 50 |
| R7 | Invoice amount exceeds the approver's limit | 5.1.1 | a control | critical | 50 |

Every anomalous invoice of this dataset is clean under policy v2.6 (`alt_label` in `labels.csv`), and every other invoice is clean under both versions.

`labels.csv` columns: `invoice_id`; `label` (clean, hard_negative, anomalous); `class_id`; `clause_ids` (the violated rules, separated by `|`); `severity`; `group_id` (invoices that form one case, such as a same-day group); `related_invoice_id` (the original that a duplicate copies); `hard_negative_type`; `note` (what was injected, in words).

## Evaluation subset

| Stratum | Invoices | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary | 150 | 32.333 |
| anomalous | 150 | 1.000 |

The weights sum to 5,000, the number of invoices. The controls and the classical detectors are evaluated on the whole ledger; the LLM configurations audit this subset, with the whole ledger as the vendor history, and their rates are computed with the weights.
